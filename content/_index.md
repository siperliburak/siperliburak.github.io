I am a second-year M.A. student in Political Science at **Sabancı University**. My research interests lie in comparative politics, political behavior, and political communication, with broader interests in political parties, public opinion, political economy, and democratic backsliding.

I study how political actors and media organizations construct competing understandings of democracy, how citizens interpret these meanings, and how those interpretations shape political attitudes and behavior. My current work examines the changing ways Turkish newspapers frame democracy, using computational text analysis and text-as-data methods.
